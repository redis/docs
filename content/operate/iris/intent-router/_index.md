Deploy Redis Intent Router on Kubernetes to give applications a service that classifies text into intents and returns the target to dispatch to.

## Self-managed

[Start with the self-managed deployment guide](/content/operate/iris/intent-router/self-managed/_index.md).

After setup, use the [developer quickstart](/content/develop/ai/context-engine/intent-router/quickstart.md) to define your first intents and match queries. The [developer guides](/content/develop/ai/context-engine/intent-router/_index.md) cover Router administration, intent authoring, and matching.
